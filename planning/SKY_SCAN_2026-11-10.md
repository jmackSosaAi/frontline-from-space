# Sky scan: can we get a second witness from space? (2026-11-10)

Access and licence check only. No analysis. Four probe lanes ran; the judge re-checked the three claims that matter most (marked "judge re-checked").

## 1. Short answer

Yes. The second sky witness can be built today with no sign-up, no payment and no licence problem. The best source is the ETH Zurich radar damage data on Zenodo: about 26.6 million buildings across all of Ukraine, each with a radar damage score for every quarter from Feb 2021 to Nov 2023, open licence (CC BY 4.0, which only asks for credit), downloads with plain curl. The catch is time: it is quarterly, not weekly or monthly, and it stops in Nov 2023. Nothing ready-made gives monthly or finer building damage. The only way to get monthly or finer is to build our own change detector from raw Sentinel-1 radar, which is also free and anonymous (Microsoft Planetary Computer gives a radar look at a given spot every 4 days or so in 2023), but that is a real build, not a download.

## 4. Recommended December plan

1. Second witness = ETH Zenodo 15088349 building damage scores (Feb 2022 to Nov 2023, quarterly).
2. Pull lat, lon and the 12 quarter scores; drop the geometry text. Assign each building to the existing H3 resolution-5 grid (the same hexagons used for fire).
3. Per cell per quarter: share of buildings whose score first crosses the damage line that quarter, using the paper's rule (damaged if the wartime peak passes the threshold and the pre-war peak did not). Threshold and the score scale are still UNVERIFIED; take them from the paper before running.
4. Roll the weekly VIINA control panel up to the same quarters (did control change in the cell, was it contested), and score damage exactly the way fire was scored. Reused: grid, panel, scoring code, settled-event tests. New: one loader and a quarter roll-up.
5. Bonus cross-check: fire vs radar on the same cells and quarters (do the two sky witnesses agree?).
6. Spot check against UNOSAT Mariupol and Kharkiv 2022 snapshots already downloaded.
7. Rough effort (judge estimate): 2 to 4 working days.
8. Honest limits: radar sees buildings that are destroyed, not strikes or fighting; quarterly steps cannot match weekly front moves; open fields and trench lines with no buildings are invisible; data ends Nov 2023, so 2024 to 2026 needs our own radar build; 2022 to 2024 has only one Sentinel-1 satellite (1B failed), so fewer looks.
9. Stretch, not December: a home-built monthly radar change detector from Planetary Computer images for a handful of front cells (Bakhmut, Avdiivka).
