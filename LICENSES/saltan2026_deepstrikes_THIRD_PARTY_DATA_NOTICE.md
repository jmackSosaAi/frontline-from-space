# Third-Party Data Notice

This project uses external data sources whose redistribution terms may differ from the licenses applied to the author's code and documentation.

The public archive therefore **does not include raw third-party files by default**. In particular, do not infer redistribution permission for:

- ACLED event exports;
- ACAPS Ukraine damage data;
- Drone Strike Map daily API/cache records;
- third-party missile/UAV source datasets;
- third-party spatial/reference data where redistribution rights are unclear.

To rebuild the full workflow, obtain the relevant source files lawfully from the original provider and place them at the paths documented in `data_raw/README_RAW_DATA.md`.

The included processed analytical outputs are deposited for research transparency and reproducibility. They do not relicense any underlying third-party source material.
